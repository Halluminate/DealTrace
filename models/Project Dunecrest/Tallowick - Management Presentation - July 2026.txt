Tallowick at a glance
A top-3 branded food and household-care manufacturer in Country B: LCB 10,612m of net sales in FY2025, 13.7% EBITDA margin, net cash

Company overview
Company overview
I Founded in 2004 by the late Hassan Tallowick as a single dairy-style dessert line, we are
today one of the three largest branded packaged-food and household-care companies in
Country B. We manufacture, market and distribute our products ourselves, from three plants
located in industrial zones around the capital.
I Our portfolio is built on the Tallowick master brand and three category brands: Dunya
(dairy-style desserts and spreads), Zeyla (biscuits and snacks) and Beyta (household care).
Dunya holds an estimated 35.2% volume share of its category, Zeyla 18.6% and Beyta
12.4%.
I In FY2025 we sold 486.4 million units and generated LCB 10,611.7m of net sales (USD
132.6m), LCB 1,449.4m of EBITDA (USD 18.1m) and a net result of LCB 855.3m. Since
2019 we have been majority-owned by Kessport Frontier Fund II, which bought out our first
institutional investor.
I We operate 11 production lines across our three plants, employ c. 2,400 people and reach
an estimated 84,000 points of sale through 46 exclusive distributors, 310 wholesalers and a
direct key-account team serving the five national modern-trade chains.

Evolution of company performance FY2021–FY2026B
Headwinds FY2021–FY2023
A prolonged disruption of the traditional trade in FY2021, when distributor credit tightened; the loss of our
founder-CEO in 2021; a 38% rise in vegetable fat, milk powder and packaging film costs between 2021 and
2023; and a 14% weakening of the LCB against the US dollar, which we could only partly pass through. Gross
margin fell from 40.1% in FY2019 to 35.5% in FY2023.
Recovery drivers FY2024–FY2026B
Normalisation of raw-material prices; the shift of the mix from value packs towards Dunya premium formats and
Zeyla family packs; and the push-and-pull commercial programme launched in 2024. EBITDA margin has
recovered to 13.7% in FY2025 and is budgeted at 15.8% in FY2026.
Source: Tallowick management accounts (unaudited) and audited financial statements FY2021–FY2025; management estimates.

A large and growing packaged-consumer market – drivers and category size

Market overview
Market drivers
I 1. Demographics – a population of 33.4 million growing at 1.4% a year, with 29% under 15; household
formation of about 165,000 a year sustains demand for family packs.
I 2. Distribution – modern trade has grown from 17% to 26% of packaged-food sales since 2020, while the
traditional trade remains the largest channel and rewards distributor coverage.
I 3. Infrastructure – the ring motorway and two new logistics platforms cut our average delivery time to
distributors from 3.1 to 1.9 days.
I 4. Women's participation – female labour-force participation rose from 34% to 38% between 2019 and
2025, lifting demand for convenience formats.
I 5. Living conditions – electrification and refrigeration penetration above 95% in urban areas support
chilled-style and ambient dessert consumption.
I 6. Disposable income – GDP per capita has grown 2.9% a year in real terms since 2021; per-capita
consumption of packaged desserts remains a third of mature-market levels.
I 7. Local innovation – consumers and retailers increasingly favour locally made brands; local producers
took 6 points of share from imports in biscuits between 2022 and 2025.
