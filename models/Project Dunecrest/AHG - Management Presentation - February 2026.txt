Overview of the Company
Largest institutional hospital group in the metro area by number of beds and facilities
Significant barriers to entry
I Brand equity and track record
– 15 years of outcomes data at AHG Central (founded 2011), a 91% patient recommendation rate
and a group brand recognised by patients, referring doctors and payers across the region.
I Economies of scale
– Group procurement saves an estimated 6-8% on consumables and pharmacy; shared back
office, a single HMIS and one radiotherapy unit serving all sites - which no single-site operator
can replicate.
I Strategic geographic coverage
– Three of four hospitals within 15 minutes of the international airport; the fourth serves the
business district and the fast-growing southern residential belt; six collection points for AHG
Diagnostics.
I Healthcare partner of choice
– Direct contracts with 46 payers (28 corporate accounts and 18 insurers); first-call provider for
evacuation and assistance companies.
Competitor facilities
AHG operates 4 hospitals and a diagnostics entity with six collection points; the six largest
local competitors are single-site operators of 118-310 beds, none with an integrated
referral network or a radiotherapy unit.
