Letter to Limited Partners
Dear Limited Partners, we are pleased to present the report of the Manager for the quarter ended 30 June
2026. As at the reporting date the Fund had drawn 92.0% of total commitments, or US$170.2M (after the
cancellation of US$20.0M of undrawn commitments in November 2022, total commitments stand at
US$185.0M). The Fund holds two remaining investments, Arvenna Health Group in Country A and
Tallowick Consumer Products in Country B, having fully realised or written off its four other investments.
Cumulative distributions to Limited Partners amount to US$68.0M, or 0.40x paid-in capital (1); the residual
value of the Fund is US$167.3M, or 0.98x paid-in capital, giving a total value to paid-in capital (TVPI) of
1.38x and a net IRR since inception of 6.6%. All valuations in this report are prepared in accordance with
the Fund's Valuation Policy (see the notes to the report) and have been reviewed by the Fund's Investment
Committee.
The net asset value of the Fund increased by US$5.7M during the quarter, from US$161.6M at 31 March
2026 to US$167.3M at 30 June 2026. The movement reflects an unrealised gain of US$5.2M on the two
remaining investments, driven by growth in last-twelve-months EBITDA at both companies with valuation
multiples unchanged, investment income and escrow accretion of US$1.0M, and fund expenses (including
the General Partner share) of US$0.5M. No capital was drawn and no distributions were made during the
quarter.
Portfolio Update
The remaining portfolio is defensive in nature, comprising a private hospital group and a branded
consumer-staples manufacturer that together represent US$153.9M of carrying value (92.0% of NAV).
Both companies delivered double-digit EBITDA growth in local currency over the last twelve months and
both are cash generative. The chart below shows the split of unrealised value by company; because each
company is the Fund's only holding in its sector and its country, the split by sector (healthcare / consumer
staples) and by country (Country A / Country B) is identical.
I Healthcare sector - Country A - Arvenna Health Group ("AHG"). Revenue for the first half of 2026 reached
US$30.3M (LCA 128.8M), 13.8% above the first half of 2025 and 98.5% of budget; on a last-twelve-months basis
to June 2026 revenue was US$58.6M against US$51.7M a year earlier. EBITDA for the half year was US$7.1M,
with the EBITDA margin broadly stable at 23.5% (23.6% in the prior-year period), reflecting higher occupancy
across the four hospitals (70.5% average versus 69.3% in 2025), growth in inpatient admissions of 1.5% to
c.18,818 for the half year and continued ramp-up of the radiotherapy unit at AHG North. Net debt stood at

Partnership Term
The Fund's initial ten-year term runs to 14 September 2027 and may be extended by up to two one-year
periods with the consent of the Limited Partner Advisory Committee, to 14 September 2029 at the latest. In
June 2025 the Committee approved the Manager's liquidation plan for the remaining portfolio and
indicated its support for both extension periods should they be needed; the Manager will make the formal
request in 2027 only if the remaining investments have not been realised by then. The Manager does not
intend to seek any extension beyond 2029 and expects to complete the liquidation of the Fund well ahead
of that date, subject to the outcome of the continuation-fund process described below.
Significant Developments
On 28 July 2026, after quarter end, the Manager gave formal notice to Limited Partners of the proposed
sale of the Fund's stakes in Arvenna Health Group and Tallowick Consumer Products to the Dunecrest
Continuation Fund, a vehicle to be managed by the Manager alongside Kessport Frontier Fund III and two
development-finance co-investors, and circulated the draft key terms; the transaction teaser ("Project
Dunecrest - Transaction Teaser") and the data room, including the transaction model, are expected to
follow in September 2026. The proposed purchase price of US$143.6M represents 93.3% of the 30 June
2026 carrying value of the two stakes (94% for AHG and 93% for Tallowick), with a targeted closing on 31
December 2026. Limited Partners will be offered the election to sell for cash or to roll into the continuation
vehicle; LPAC conflict clearance has been sought and an independent fairness opinion commissioned.
Further detail is set out in the section "Proposed Continuation-Fund Transaction".
Administrative Updates
The Fund's audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 were issued on 24 April
2026 with an unqualified opinion. The annual general meeting of Limited Partners was held virtually on 9
June 2026. The escrow of US$2.05M retained from the 2023 sale of Company 4 was released in full on 17
July 2026 and will be distributed together with the Tallowick dividend in the fourth quarter. The Manager
will not draw further capital other than to meet fund expenses, and the remaining undrawn commitment of
US$14.8M is expected to be cancelled in part at the liquidation of the Fund.
We thank you for your continued support and remain available to discuss any aspect of this report. Yours
faithfully, Kessport Frontier Managers Limited, Manager.

Background of Arvenna Health Group
Arvenna Health Group was formed in 2019 by Dr. Karim Sfaxi-Mellouli, its Founder and Chairman, as a
hospital owner-operator to address the persistent undersupply of private hospital beds in Country A, where
the ratio of beds to population is less than half the level of comparable middle-income countries. The
platform's first asset was AHG Central, the 176-bed flagship hospital in the capital region, followed by AHG
Coast (120 beds) and AHG North (142 beds, including the group's oncology and radiotherapy unit), both
acquired through AHG Central as parent. The Fund invested in July 2021 to fund the fourth acquisition, the

Background of Tallowick Consumer Products
Company description and investment rationale
Tallowick was founded in 2004 as a family company by its late founder, starting with a single ambient
dairy-style dessert line under the Dunya name, and has grown over twenty-two years into one of the three
largest branded packaged-food and household-care manufacturers in Country B. The company
manufactures, markets and distributes its products itself from three plants (eleven production lines) in
industrial zones around the capital, employs about 2,400 people and reaches an estimated 84,000 points
of sale through 46 exclusive distributors (41 in 2023), some 310 wholesalers and a direct key-account
team serving the five national modern-trade chains. Its portfolio comprises the Tallowick master brand and
three category brands: Dunya (dairy-style desserts and spreads, with an estimated 35% volume share of
its category), Zeyla (biscuits and snacks) and Beyta (household care). At the time of the Fund's investment
in November 2019, when the Fund bought out the previous institutional investor and subscribed new
shares for a 74% stake, the packaged-food market in Country B was projected to grow at 7% to 9% per
annum; the investment thesis rested on a large and fast-growing consumer market, a founding family and
management team retaining a 26% stake and operational leadership, and the professionalisation of
management, which has since been strengthened under Yacine Boudiaf (Chief Operating Officer from
2017 and CEO since 2021, following the death of the founder), with Nadia Kherbache as CFO, Samir
Ould-Amar as Commercial Director and Farid Aklouche-Sebti as Operations Director.

and 7.0x for Tallowick Consumer Products, unchanged from 31 March 2026. Valuations were reviewed by
the Investment Committee on 21 July 2026.

operating performance from currency movements; the FX line in each NAV bridge captures the translation

Fund Status section) with the residual value of US$167.3M as a terminal inflow at 30 June 2026, net of all
fees, expenses and carried interest (nil). Gross IRR and gross MoC are computed on investment-level
cash flows before fees, expenses and carried interest.

capital. Carried interest is calculated on a whole-fund basis under LPA clause 12; the preferred return has
not been met and no carried interest is accrued. Fund expenses of US$0.5M for the quarter include
US$0.14M of legal and professional costs relating to the proposed continuation-fund transaction.
