Executive Summary
Kessport Frontier proposes a continuation vehicle to acquire Fund II's last two portfolio companies and offer Fund II LPs a liquidity or roll election
Kessport Frontier Managers Limited
STRICTLY PRIVATE AND CONFIDENTIAL
Project Dunecrest – Transaction Teaser vF   |   5
Transaction Overview
Transaction Summary
I Kessport Frontier Managers Limited (the "Sponsor") is pursuing a multi-asset transaction (the
"Transaction" or "Project Dunecrest") for the last two remaining investments of Kessport Frontier Fund
II ("Fund II"): Arvenna Health Group ("AHG") and Tallowick Consumer Products ("Tallowick", together
the "Companies" or the "Portfolio").
I A newly formed vehicle, Dunecrest Continuation Fund (the "Continuation Vehicle" or "CV"), will
acquire Fund II's 58% interest in AHG and 74% interest in Tallowick at a purchase price of $143.6M
(93.3% of the $153.9M Reference Date NAV), alongside $10.2M of committed follow-on capital for the
add-on programmes of both Companies.
I Total CV commitments of $167.7M are assumed, comprising $145.1M of funded capital at closing
(purchase price plus $1.4M of buy-side transaction fees), $10.2M of follow-on equity and $12.4M of
management fees and expenses drawn over the life of the CV.
I Fund II limited partners may elect to sell for cash, roll their interest into the CV on the same terms as
new investors, or a combination; the base case assumes 41% of the funded capital is rolled ($59.5M),
$5.2M of GP economics is reinvested, the GP commits $2.9M out of pocket and $77.5M is provided by
new investors.

Transaction Objectives
I 1. Provide optional liquidity to Fund II investors and wind up the fund
– Fund II (2017 vintage) is in its tenth year; the two remaining assets represent the bulk of remaining
NAV and cannot be realised at full value within the fund term.
I 2. Extend the holding period to unlock the upside in both Companies
– AHG's radiotherapy build-out, bed expansion and add-on pipeline and Tallowick's premium-mix
migration are expected to lift combined EBITDA from $36.9M in FY2026E to $55.2M in FY2030E.
I 3. Acquire the interests at a modest discount to NAV
– Purchase prices at 94% (AHG) and 93% (Tallowick) of Reference Date NAV, with NAV set at 8.0x
and 7.0x LTM Q2'26 EBITDA respectively; co-investors (Fund III, DFI Partner 1, DFI Partner 2,
Tallowick management) retain their stakes.
I 4. Provide unfunded capital for growth
– $10.2M of follow-on equity is committed pro rata to the CV ownership to fund the identified
acquisitions; other shareholders fund their share.

Transaction Overview
Transaction Description
I A. Continuation Vehicle
– Dunecrest Continuation Fund, a newly formed limited partnership managed by the Sponsor, acquires
Fund II's interests in AHG and Tallowick at closing (31 December 2026) and commits follow-on
capital.
– Capitalised by the GP's out-of-pocket commitment ($2.9M, 2% of funded capital), reinvested GP

I B. New Limited Partners
– Acquire the interests of selling Fund II LPs net of fees and expenses; base case $77.5M of funded
capital (53.4%).
– Same terms, same fee and carry as rolling LPs; the CV constitutes a single class of interests.

– Election period of 20 business days after the Fund II LPAC status-quo option is confirmed: (i) sell for
cash at the purchase price, (ii) roll into the CV, or (iii) a combination.
– Fund II is wound up after closing; escrows from prior exits are distributed under the existing LPA.

LP Election Mechanics, Governance and Alignment
Fund II LPs choose between cash and a roll on identical terms; the GP commits $8.1M of its own capital and economics

Transaction Overview
LP Election Mechanics
I Status quo option: the Fund II LPAC will confirm that LPs may sell, roll or split their interest; no LP is
required to roll and no LP is disadvantaged by rolling (identical fee, carry and governance terms to new
investors).
I Cash option: selling LPs receive their pro rata share of the purchase price at closing, less their share of
Fund II transaction costs (capped at 0.5% of proceeds).
I Roll option: rolling LPs contribute their Fund II interest in kind and receive CV interests at the same
price per unit as new LPs; rolled capital counts towards net invested capital for the management fee.
I Base case: 41.0% of funded capital is rolled ($59.5M); a roll rate below 25% would be back-stopped by
the new investors on a pro rata basis.
I Election timetable: election forms are circulated on 30 October 2026 after the second-round price is
confirmed; the window closes on 27 November 2026.

Continuation Vehicle Returns Analysis
I Concentration
– The CV holds two assets in two frontier markets; a shortfall at either Company has a direct effect on returns.
I Currency
– All revenue and costs are in LCA and LCB; a 10% depreciation of both currencies against the USD would reduce
the modelled exit proceeds by roughly the same proportion absent local inflation pass-through.
I Exit timing and multiple
– The base case assumes M&A exits in December 2031 at 8.5x and 7.5x; exit markets for regional assets are
shallow and may require a longer hold or a lower multiple.
I Regulatory and tariff
– Private hospital tariffs and insurance reimbursement in Country A are subject to periodic regulation; price controls
on staple foods in Country B may limit pass-through of input costs.
I Execution of add-ons
– $10.2M of follow-on capital is committed to acquisitions that are not yet signed; unspent commitments reduce
returns through fees on undrawn capital only marginally, but delayed integration could weigh on EBITDA.
I Conflicts of interest
– The Sponsor manages both Fund II (seller) and the CV (buyer); mitigated by LPAC approval, an independent
fairness opinion and the LP election.
I Minority protections
– The CV holds 58% of AHG alongside Fund III and two DFIs and 74% of Tallowick alongside management;
shareholder agreements contain reserved matters that require co-investor consent.
I Key person and management
– Both Companies depend on founder-led management teams; retention plans and succession planning are in
place but not guaranteed.
I Macroeconomic and political
– Both countries face inflation, current-account pressure and periodic political change (see the Macro update in the
VDR).
I Projections
– The projections are the Sponsor's base case derived from the Transaction Model and management budgets;
actual results will differ and may differ materially.
